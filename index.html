Meet the Little Monsters

Three eight-year-olds. One very strange friendship.

01 🦇

THE DRAMATIC TWIN

Victor Ravencroft

A little vampire with messy black hair, tiny fangs, and a flair for the dramatic. Unfortunately for Victor, nobody else seems to take his crises seriously.

02 ☾

THE QUIET TWIN

Violet Ravencroft

Quiet, sweet, and wonderfully mysterious. With her long black hair covering her eyes, Violet has a talent for staying calm while her brother completely loses it.

03 💀

THE SARCASTIC NEIGHBOR

Skellie DeBones

A laid-back skeleton who loves skateboarding, drawing creepy creatures, and making sarcastic remarks. Especially when Victor is around.

About Woeful Studio

Woeful Studio is the creative home of Victor & Violet, an original spooky-cute gothic comedy webcomic.

Inspired by quirky humor, unusual friendships, and the charmingly strange, the series follows the everyday adventures of two little vampires and their skeleton friend.
